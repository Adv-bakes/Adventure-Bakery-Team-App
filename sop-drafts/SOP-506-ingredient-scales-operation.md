*Machines: two digital bench/platform scales used to weigh ingredients — an **OHAUS Defender 3000**
(i-DT33 indicator) and a **second bench scale** (green display / blue keypad — make & model to be
confirmed). Both are operated the same way: reach a stable zero, tare the container, add ingredient to
the target weight, and read only when the reading is stable.*

---

## Purpose

To weigh ingredients **accurately to the batch sheet**.

**Two things drive the rules below.** The weight decides the formula — a scale that reads wrong means
wrong quantities, which is a **formulation and allergen risk** (too much or too little of an ingredient,
including allergens). And the platform **contacts food and food containers**, so it must be kept clean
and free of foreign matter.

## Scope

Production staff weighing ingredients on the bench scales.

**Calibration** (adjusting the scale against certified reference weights) is done by QA or an approved
calibration provider on a schedule — **not by operators**. If a scale reads wrong or is damaged,
operators **tag it out and report it** rather than adjust it.

## Responsibility

- **Operators** — level/clean check, power on to a stable zero, confirm the unit, tare and weigh to the
  batch-sheet target, keep the platform clean, and **tag out and report** a scale that reads wrong or is
  damaged.
- **Supervisor / QA** — maintain the scale calibration program and the equipment directory, arrange
  periodic calibration, and resolve an out-of-calibration scale and any product weighed on it.

## Procedure

### Before you start

- The scale sits on a **level, stable, vibration-free** surface; the **platform is clean, dry, and clear**
  of foreign matter, spilled product, and debris.
- Power on: press and hold **On/Zero** (about 2 seconds) and let the scale settle to a **stable zero**.
  If it doesn't sit at zero with an empty platform, press **Zero** (must be stable).
- Confirm the **weighing unit** (kg / g / lb / oz) matches the batch sheet — press and hold **Units** to
  change it. Weighing in the wrong unit is a common error.

### 1. Weigh into a container (tare)

- Place the **empty container** on the platform and press **Tare** — the display returns to **zero (net)**
  and stores the container weight.
- Add the ingredient until the display reaches the **target weight** on the batch sheet. Read the value
  only when the **stable indicator** is showing (the reading has settled).
- Use a **food-safe container**, don't **overload past the scale's capacity**, and keep the load centered
  on the platform.
- To clear the tare, remove everything and press **Tare** again (display returns to gross zero).

### 2. Weighing to a target

- Read only a **stable** reading; a drifting number is not a valid weight.
- On the OHAUS Defender, **Check** mode (if set up) shows **green when the weight is in the target range**,
  yellow under, red over — a quick visual confirm. It does not replace reading the number.
- Double-check the **unit** and the **target** against the batch sheet before you commit the ingredient.

### 3. Allergen care

- **Clean the platform and the container between allergen ingredients.** Brush or wipe spilled powder off
  the platform so it isn't carried into the next ingredient (allergen cross-contact).

### 4. After weighing and cleaning

- Power off with a long press of **Off** (or leave on if auto-off is set).
- **Disconnect / unplug before wet cleaning.** Wipe the platform and surround with a clean damp cloth;
  **do not let water into the indicator** or connections. Remove and clean the pan if fitted.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| Won't hold zero | Remove all weight, make sure the platform is clean/dry and the scale is stable, then press **Zero**. |
| Reading drifts or won't stabilize | Check for draft, vibration, a wet or overloaded platform, or something touching the pan; fix and re-check. |
| Reading looks wrong or you doubt its accuracy | **Tag "Do Not Use", weigh on another scale, and tell the Supervisor.** Do not adjust the scale — QA verifies it against certified weights. |
| Scale dropped, knocked, or overloaded | Tag it out and have QA verify it before it is used again. |
| Wrong unit shown | Press and hold **Units** until the batch-sheet unit appears. |

## Records

**Calibration certificates / records** from the periodic calibration are kept by QA, and a **directory of
measuring equipment** that requires calibration is maintained (SQF 11.2.3.6). Operator training sign-off
is held in the training record. All retained per the record retention policy.
