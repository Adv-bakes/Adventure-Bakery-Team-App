---

## Purpose

To run the Kook-E-King cookie depositor safely and get consistent, correctly spaced cookies.

The one food-safety risk that is specific to this machine is the **cut-off wire**. It is a thin
steel wire under tension and it can break during a run. A broken wire is metal in the product, so
every rule below about checking the wire and holding product when it breaks matters.

## Scope

Production staff setting up, running, or unloading the depositor.

Cleaning is **SOP-902**. Motor/gear-reducer/chain lubrication and mechanical adjustment (wire lift
cam, scrapers, chain tension) are Maintenance — not operators.

## Responsibility

- **Operators** — set up and run the machine as written; stop and report anything unusual.
- **Supervisor** — trains and signs off operators; takes the machine out of service on a fault;
  signs the pre-use release on FRM-910.
- **Maintenance** — lubrication, wire-lift/cam/scraper/chain adjustment, and repair, with the power
  unplugged.

## Procedure

### Before you start

**Don't operate this machine unless the Supervisor has trained and signed you off.**

Check, **with the machine unplugged**:

- FRM-910 is signed for this shift — if it isn't, the machine hasn't been released for production
- The **die** is seated in its slot and the six thumbscrews are **finger-tight only** (over-tightening
  warps the head and leaks dough)
- The **cut-off wire** is present, not frayed or kinked, and there is **one support finger in each
  die slot**, with the wire sitting in the small slots at the finger tips
- **Both safety guards** (side guard and gear guard) are closed and locked — the machine will not run
  with a guard open
- Hopper is secure on its hooks; power cord and plug are undamaged
- Pans, table belts, and the out-feed area are clean

Anything fails → don't run it. Tag it and tell the Supervisor.

**Get yourself ready:** sleeves down and secured, no dangling ties or lanyards, **no rings, watches
or bracelets**, hair fully in a hairnet. Keep hands and clothing clear of the feed rollers, the wire,
and the moving pans.

### 1. Set up

1. **Unplug the machine.**
2. Confirm a die is in the slot and the wire support fingers are aligned to the die slots (above).
3. Place an 18" sheet pan under the feed area; pull the pan extension out fully at the out-feed end.
4. Set **Deposit Speed**, **Table Speed** and **Cut-Off Speed** all to **1** (slowest).
5. Set the **Cut-Off Timer** switch to **On**.

### 2. Load

Load dough into the hopper. Spread it **end to end along the rollers**, not heaped in the middle, and
don't pile it against the hopper sides — dough hanging on the sides feeds unevenly.

Feed dough straight from the mixer where you can. Keep pens, tape, thermometers and spare utensils
away from the open hopper.

### 3. Start and dial it in

1. **Plug the machine in.**
2. Press the **green Power-On** button at the front. The rollers turn and the pan travels forward.
   **Watch it — be ready to hit a red Stop button** if anything binds or catches.
3. Adjust while running (all three knobs adjust live):
   - **Cookie size / thickness** — Deposit Speed + Cut-Off Speed together
   - **Spacing down the pan** — faster Cut-Off drops them closer; faster Table Speed spreads them apart
4. Record your settings on the batch sheet / settings chart so setup is faster next time.

Two red Stop buttons are on top of the cabinet; the green button restarts.

### 4. Watch the cut-off wire

- Glance at the wire regularly. **If the wire breaks or a piece is missing:**
  1. **Stop the machine** (red button) and **unplug it**.
  2. **Hold every pan back to the last confirmed-good wire check** — treat it as possible metal in
     product; segregate and tag it, and tell the Supervisor and Quality.
  3. Account for **all** of the broken wire before anything restarts. Fit a new wire assembly
     (Maintenance) before running again.
- If the wire keeps breaking, the machine may be out of alignment or need a different wire — stop and
  get Maintenance; don't just keep replacing it.

### 5. Refill / short stops

When the hopper runs low: press a **red Stop** button, **unplug**, refill the hopper, then press the
**green** button to restart.

### 6. Shut down

To stop for cleaning: run the machine until the **wire is fully clear of the die in its furthest-back
position**, press **Stop**, **unplug**, and hand over to sanitation — **SOP-902**.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| Nothing runs | Check the cord is plugged in and **both guards are closed** (an open guard stops it). |
| Everything runs except the wire cut-off | Check the Cut-Off Timer switch; check the control-panel fuse. If it persists, get Maintenance. |
| Dough leaks out the back of the rollers | Die thumbscrews too tight — loosen to finger-tight. Check the hopper is seated and clean underneath. Otherwise it's a scraper adjustment — Maintenance. |
| Cookies uneven / different sizes on a row | Spread dough end-to-end, don't overfill the hopper, don't run refrigerated dough. Center cookie off = over-tight die thumbscrews. |
| **Wire breaks / piece missing** | **Stop, unplug, hold product back to the last good wire check, account for all the wire, tell Supervisor + Quality.** (Section 4.) |
| Odd noise, grinding, binding | Stop immediately, unplug, get Maintenance. |

## Lubricant

The manual calls for **"a couple of drops of mineral oil on the brass bushings"** at the feed-roller
shaft ends on reassembly. Those bushings sit right by the rollers that push the dough, so a
**food-safe (NSF H1) oil** is used there — only food-safe oil is used on this machine. This is what
satisfies **SQF 11.2.1.7** (food-grade lubricant on/over food contact).
