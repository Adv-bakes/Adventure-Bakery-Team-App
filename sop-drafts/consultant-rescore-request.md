Hello,

Thank you for the gap assessment. We are working through the findings and have built the

While going through the file we found four rows where the **evidence recorded in the file

## 1. Clause 2.1.1.1 — Food safety policy

The requirement lists **six** parts, i to vi. Parts v and vi require the policy to be signed
by the senior site manager, displayed in prominent positions, and effectively communicated
to all site personnel in the language(s) they understand.

The evidence recorded is:

> "The written Food Safety Policy complies with 2.1.1. i-iv requirements."

The evidence itself claims four of six parts. FSQM-002 as assessed contained no signature
block, no display provision and no language provision.

*(We have since revised FSQM-002 to add all three, so the underlying gap is closed. The
scoring is what we are asking you to correct.)*

Minor note on the same row: the effective date is recorded as "October 28, 20225".

## 2. Clause 2.3.4.6 — Supplier audits based on risk

> "Adventure Bakery has not done a complete evaluation of the suppliers to determine whether
> audits need to be performed."

The clause requires supplier audits to be based on risk as determined in 2.3.4.2. The
evidence states that the determination has not been made.

## 3. Clause 2.4.3.15 — Verification of CCP monitoring

> "Verification procedures have all the required elements, although some of the CCPs are not
> appropriate."

The clause requires procedures verifying that critical control points are effectively
monitored. If some CCPs are not appropriate, we do not see how the verification of those
CCPs can be effective.

We would also value the detail behind this one, since it matters to us regardless of the
score: **which** CCPs are not appropriate, and why. That feeds directly into our HACCP plan
rebuild.

## 4. Clause 2.4.3.17 — Regulatory methodology

> "Although the Food Safety Plan has not been developed following the applicable US
> regulatory requirements (21 CFR 117 - Preventive Controls for Human Foods Rule), Adventure
> Bakery might fall under the rule exemption."

The recommendation recorded against the same row reads:

> "Ensure that Adventure Bakery falls under the exemption of the Preventive Controls for
> Human Foods Rule (21 CFR 117) The organization should have an exemption letter from the
> FDA."

A row scored Compliant on the basis of an exemption that is described as unconfirmed, with a
recommendation to obtain evidence that does not yet exist, reads as a finding rather than a
compliance. We do not hold an FDA exemption letter.
