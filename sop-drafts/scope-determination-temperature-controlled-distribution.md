## Basis

All finished product is shipped and stored by the customer at **ambient** temperature.

The one product that required frozen distribution was **discontinued on `[DATE — to be
confirmed]`**. No temperature-controlled product has been produced since, and none is planned.

This is a determination about **distribution only**. It does not narrow any other part of the
system — see *What this does not cover* below.

## What this does not cover

**Temperature-controlled storage remains fully in scope.** The site operates two monitored units in
daily use:

| Unit | Operating range observed | Monitoring |
|---|---|---|
| Walk-In Refrigerator | 34–41 °F | Continuous, YoLink sensor |
| Walk-In Freezer | −10 to 13 °F | Continuous, YoLink sensor |

SQF **11.6.2.1, 11.6.2.2 and 11.6.2.3** therefore apply and are addressed by the temperature-
controlled storage procedure, not by this determination. Records are held in the Team Portal
(`/team/compliance/temperature`).

Ambient distribution also does not remove the rest of **11.6.5** — vehicle inspection, load
security, tamper seals and dock practice all still apply to ambient loads.

## When this determination is void

It lapses immediately, and 11.6.5.5–.7 return to scope, if the site:

- reintroduces the discontinued frozen product, or
- develops, trials or accepts any product requiring chilled or frozen distribution, or
- ships any product under temperature control for a customer, however occasionally.

The trigger point is **product intake and development** — a PRF or specification that states a
chilled or frozen distribution condition must raise this determination for review before the
product is accepted. That linkage belongs in the Change Management Procedure.

## Evidence to align before the audit

1. **The shelf-life records still say "Frozen, 18 months"** — four records in the Team Portal. They
   relate to the discontinued product. Left as they are, they contradict this determination in
   front of an auditor. Mark them as historical, or attach them to the discontinued product so the
   record and the scope statement tell the same story.
2. **The discontinuation date must be filled in and supportable.** "Recently" is not an answer to an
   auditor, and the product was made inside the audit window, so production and distribution records
   for it still exist and will be seen. State the date and let the records corroborate it.
3. If the frozen product was made under a customer specification that is still open, the
   specification should be closed or marked withdrawn.
